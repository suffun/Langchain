Cricket

Cricket is a game of skill and might,  
Played with passion from morn till night.  
The bowler runs with all his speed,  
The batter waits for the perfect feed.  

The ball flies fast across the ground,  
Excited cheers rise all around.  
A stunning shot races for four,  
The roaring crowd demands some more.  

A mighty six climbs through the sky,  
While fielders watch the ball fly high.  
A wicket falls, the cheers explode,  
The players race along the road.  

The keeper stands behind the sticks,  
Waiting for catches, fast and quick.  
One brilliant catch can change the game,  
And make a player rise to fame.  

Cricket teaches courage, teamwork, and pride,  
It brings millions of hearts side by side.  
Win or lose, we give our best,  
Because cricket beats within our chest. 🏏
